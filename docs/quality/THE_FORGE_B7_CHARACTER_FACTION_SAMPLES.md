# THE FORGE B7 — Character Faction generated quality packet

## Method and scope

Generated/content-author review by Codex, not independent human playtest or native-app acceptance. Reproducible command: `cargo run -p forge-app-core --example character_faction_samples`. Four source seeds (0–3) for each of ten non-Chaos presets, plus four Chaos Blend sources; action seed = 100 + source seed. Neutral axes, Character content v2, Faction context recipe v1.

All 44 final outputs were reviewed for visible Character relevance, coherent profile/identity/goal/methods/culture, source contradictions, complete clones and raw identity/hash leakage. Each includes its exact source facts and all 13 Faction fields below. Contextual source facts are quoted deliberately; trimming quotations and giving the joint undertaking a specific name is a minor editorial improvement, not evidence of a deterministic or persistence defect.

Results: 44/44 visibly use faction_hint in Identity, desire in Goal and secret in Hidden Truth; 44 distinct complete creative bodies; zero raw UUID/hash leakage in the generated prose. Names can repeat from the accepted standalone name corpus; repeated names are not complete clones. The hint is not forced into Name.

The initial review caught random profile fallback for ambiguous hints (for example a kingdom around a cooperative). This was repaired in the new contextual path: explicit institutional hints guide Profile; ambiguous intent stays Other. The final packet is regenerated after that correction. Standalone Faction content is unchanged.

Chaos samples retain mixed established corpus methods/resources/symbols. Their shared Character intent supplies the causal thread; a human author may further align the secondary imagery. No direct unexplained contradiction was found. Specific plausible tensions are recorded per sample. No B7 P0/P1/P2 finding remains.
