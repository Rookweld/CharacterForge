# THE FORGE B4 — Item sample packet

## Method and review boundary

**Generated/content-author review by Codex, not independent human playtesting or operator acceptance.** Reproduce with `cargo run -p forge-app-core --example item_samples`. Item generator/content versions are 1/1; all Creative Axes are neutral (50). The 40 non-Chaos samples use seeds 0–3 for each of ten presets; Chaos uses 10000–10011. All 13 canonical fields are retained below.

The review inspected physical form, profile, purpose/function/drawback, provenance fiction, scarcity, condition, secret leverage, and actionable hooks. Each record below has a classification. Ratings: **22 usable as-is, 30 minor edit, 0 weak**. These are author judgments, not measured player outcomes. Minor edits principally connect independently selected custody events and secondary hook leads, or specify how a broad repair tool implements its function. Chaos often needs a setting bridge between its source cultures.

No exact full-content clones, missing fields, malformed output, or unresolved severe contradiction was observed in the final packet. Repeated names, profile capability sentences, and adjacent hook groups do occur; the corpus has six alternatives per selection layer, rather than unlimited vocabulary. The three hooks are proposals, not assertions that linked entities already exist. Optional author editing can narrow several leads to one plot. Generic object form is intentionally combined with preset-specific materials, commissioning circumstances, history, secrets, and hooks.

During author review, ranged weapon forms with melee text were corrected to compatible melee forms; wearable Armor forms were kept compatible with wearable drawbacks; Consumable properties/condition now refer explicitly to protective packaging where needed. Purposes were expanded into six distinct roles per profile. Standalone hooks referring to an unnamed “that family” or “that destination” were made self-contained. These repairs preceded final verification.
