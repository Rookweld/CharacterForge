# The Forge B3 — Faction generated sample review

This packet contains 52 generated Factions: four from each of the ten non-Chaos presets and twelve from Chaos Blend. It was regenerated from the checked-in `faction_samples` example after the content review fixes. The sample seeds are 0–3 for each non-Chaos preset and 10000–10011 for Chaos Blend. Generator version 1; content version 1; neutral Creative Axes.

This is a generated/content-author review, not an independent human playtest. The preset sweep separately checks 256 seeds per preset for validity, deterministic replay, and exact full-payload content duplicates; the Chaos sweep checks 1,024 seeds and source-group diversity. The sweeps found no exact full Faction clones. Chaos uses three deterministic, seed-derived source groups: name/identity/ideology/goal/methods; culture/resources/public face; hidden truth/symbols/reputation/hooks. All ten eligible source presets appeared in each group across the sweep.

Review covered full clones, malformed prose, contradictions, severe repetition, preset identity, and coherence among identity, ideology, goal, methods, culture, public face, and hidden truth. The reviewed samples had distinct institutional forms for the 13 profiles, recognizable preset vocabulary, and actionable hooks. Cross-field combinations can still need small editorial alignment because fields are independently rerollable. Review classification: **40 usable as-is, 12 usable with minor editing, 0 weak**. No unresolved severe content defect was observed.
